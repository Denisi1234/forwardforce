REQUEST A TEMPLATE

Six rules for print-ready artwork

Following these will help your job go to print faster, with no surprises.

Add bleed

Extend background colours and images 3 mm beyond the trim edge so no white lines appear after cutting.

Respect the safe zone

Keep text and logos at least 3-5 mm inside the trim edge.

Use high resolution

Images should be 300 dpi at the final print size (100-150 dpi is fine for large banners).

Design in CMYK

Screens use RGB; printers use CMYK. Convert your file to CMYK to avoid colour shifts.

Outline your fonts

Convert text to outlines/curves, or embed fonts in your PDF, so your text prints exactly as designed.

Send the right file

PDF, AI, EPS or SVG are best. High-quality PNG/JPG are accepted. We always send a proof before printing.

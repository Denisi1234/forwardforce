Quality At Every Step

How we ensure consistency from raw artwork files to finished physical goods.

Step 1

Pre-Flight Check

Every artwork file is verified for resolution, CMYK profile, bleed margins, and safe zones before hitting the press.

Step 2

Sample & Run Inspection

Trained press technicians monitor registration, thread density, and ink cure times throughout the entire production run.

Step 3

Final QC & Pack

Each batch is hand-checked for defects, counted, and securely packed to ensure crisp, undamaged arrival at your door.
